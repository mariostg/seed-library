Thank you very much for your gift to the Ottawa Wildflower Seed Library! Your donation will help us continue to provide free native seeds and plants to communities.

Help us continue to maintain habitat corridors for our wildlife – donate your native wildflower seeds!

Because of donors like you, since 2020, over 15,000 community projects, schools and individuals have received free native seeds in Ontario, Québec, New Brunswick, and Nova Scotia. Over 80 cents of your donated dollar goes directly to the cost of postage – which leapt from $1.07 when we started in 2020 to a projected $2 in 2027.

The Ottawa Wildflower Seed Library disseminates accessible scientific information about native plants, the ecosystem, and good ecological practices. We have an extensive catalogue containing each species that we share. Our outreach includes an impressive social media presence and presentations and workshops across the Ottawa-Gatineau region. We have been regularly reaching more than 2,000 people each year, making education on the importance of native plants in ecosystems a core part of our impact.

More broadly, your financial donation will allow us to continue engaging with communities to fulfill our stewardship role in nature, fight against biodiversity loss, create resilient ecosystems in the face of climate change, and foster Indigenous reconciliation. We welcome both one-time and monthly donations, to maintain our operations year- round.

Thank you once again for your generous contribution. Please feel free to share the Ottawa Wildflower Seed Library with your networks to sustain this mailout operation into the future.
